THE STORY BEHIND THE PROJECT

Built around a real person’s need.

The problem

My brother, known on social media as TheBrownShirtGuy, wanted a way to stay connected with his followers beyond a single social account. An email audience would give him another channel to reach people if he lost access to that account.

Where I started

After he showed me a newsletter page he had made with a website service, I challenged myself to build an alternative in HTML and CSS. I began by recreating the basic layout, then explored ways to improve it rather than stopping at the first working page.

My contribution and process

I built the initial page, revised the layout and styling, and worked through my brother’s specific feedback about placement and presentation. I asked my instructor for help with CSS and troubleshooting when I got stuck, including showing the actual page when a verbal explanation was not enough. After taking a break, I returned with new ideas and continued refining the design.

What changed

The project grew into two design approaches for the same newsletter goal. The editorial design explores a split layout and story-led presentation; the stacked design uses a vertical reading flow and a centred signup card. Further implementation, testing and portfolio preparation included AI assistance.

What I learned

I practised turning feedback into concrete layout changes, debugging small styling problems, asking focused questions and revisiting work with fresh ideas. The experience taught me that making a page work and making it communicate clearly are separate steps.

Current result

Both designs are available as public frontend demonstrations. The source also includes a local signup backend and newsletter dashboard. Live email delivery still needs configuration and testing; no audience growth, production launch or subscriber results are claimed.

Both public demos validate sample input without creating subscriptions or sending email. Stacked Design also includes a separate local Node.js/Express backend and newsletter dashboard; live email delivery requires configuration and testing.
